⚡ Tech

🛰️ Nova Spectral AI raises Rs 21.15 crore for infrared sensor chips

Bengaluru startup Nova Spectral AI raised Rs 21.15 crore in a seed round led by Shastra VC, with Equirus InnovateX Fund and angels also participating. It makes lead-free colloidal quantum dot short-wave infrared sensor chips on standard silicon CMOS for defence surveillance and physical AI uses, with lower-cost imaging in low light, haze and smoke.

Why it matters: Defence and robotics firms may get cheaper infrared sensors that work in harder visibility conditions.

🇮🇳 India

🗳️ Karnataka final voter roll drops by nearly one crore

Karnataka published its final electoral roll after the Special Intensive Revision, with 4,55,00,011 voters, down from 5,54,32,314 before the exercise. The Chief Electoral Officer said the roll includes 2,25,40,493 men, 2,29,12,355 women and 2,841 third-gender electors. More than 6.83 lakh voters are in the 18-19 age group.

Why it matters: The Supreme Court has granted petitioners ten days to file a rejoinder.

Original: Indian Express ↗
🇮🇳 India

⚖️ Bombay High Court pauses FRRO leave-India notice to Imran Khan

The Bombay High Court put on hold the Foreigners Regional Registration Office’s leave-India notice to actor Imran Khan until November 2. The FRRO issued the notice over an alleged violation of the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025, after a complaint by BJP leader Kirit Somaiya and a police-forwarded FIR. Khan has challenged the notice as unconstitutional and disproportionate.

Why it matters: The next hearing is set for November 2, with no action on the notice until then.

📈 Markets

💰 Indian lenders delay $2.5 billion dollar debt sales

Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank and Bank of India had planned to raise up to $2.5 billion through dollar debt using the central bank’s subsidised hedging facility. Bankers said they have now shelved the issuances because higher US yields, a weaker rupee and more expensive hedging made dollar funding costlier than rupee borrowing. India’s lenders have already raised $13 billion under the scheme.

Why it matters: Banks may wait for cheaper foreign funding before using the RBI facility.

✈️ Tokoname’s Flight of Dreams turns a Boeing 787 prototype into a park

Flight of Dreams in Tokoname, Japan, was originally designed by TeamLab and is centered on the first Boeing 787 prototype, ZA-001. Why it matters: Visitors can see the first Boeing 787 prototype inside an airport attraction.
