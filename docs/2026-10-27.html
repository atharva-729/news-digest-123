Bengaluru startup Nova Spectral AI raised Rs 21.15 crore in a seed round led by Shastra VC, with Equirus InnovateX Fund and angels participating. It builds lead-free short-wave infrared image sensor chips on standard silicon CMOS for defence ISR, or intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, plus robots and autonomous vehicles.

Why it matters: Cheaper infrared sensors could broaden where drones, surveillance systems and robots can see in poor conditions.

🇮🇳 India

🗳️ Karnataka voter roll falls by nearly 1 crore after revision

Karnataka’s final electoral roll now has 4,55,00,011 voters, down 99.32 lakh from before the Special Intensive Revision. The Chief Electoral Officer said the roll includes 2,25,40,493 men, 2,29,12,355 women and 2,841 third-gender electors, with more than 6.83 lakh voters aged 18-19.

Why it matters: The shrinkage shows how the revision is changing the size and makeup of voter lists.

Original: Indian Express ↗
🇮🇳 India

⚖️ Bombay High Court pauses leave-India notice to actor Imran Khan

The Bombay High Court put on hold the Foreigners Regional Registration Office’s leave-India notice to actor Imran Khan until November 2. The notice came after a complaint by BJP leader Kirit Somaiya and a police FIR tied to Khan’s participation in a protest against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

Why it matters: Khan will not face action on the notice until the court hears the case again on November 2.
