🔬 Nova Spectral AI raises Rs 21.15 crore for infrared sensor chips

Bengaluru startup Nova Spectral AI raised Rs 21.15 crore in seed funding led by Shastra VC, with Equirus InnovateX Fund and angels. It builds lead-free short-wave infrared image sensor chips on standard silicon for defence, robots, drones and other physical AI uses.

Why it matters: Cheaper infrared sensors could widen the use of computer vision in defence and autonomous systems.

🇮🇳 India

🗳️ Karnataka’s final voter roll drops by nearly 1 crore after SIR

Karnataka published its final electoral roll after the Special Intensive Revision, with 4,55,00,011 voters, down from 5,54,32,314. The revision also led to more than 43.5 lakh notices, and the Supreme Court is hearing challenges to the nationwide SIR process and to changes in Form 6.

Why it matters: Karnataka’s roll is now smaller, and the court case could shape how electoral-roll revisions are handled elsewhere.

Original: Indian Express ↗
🇮🇳 India

⚖️ Bombay High Court pauses FRRO notice to actor Imran Khan

The Bombay High Court put on hold a Foreigners Regional Registration Office notice asking actor Imran Khan to leave India until Monday, November 2. Khan is challenging the notice; the FRRO issued it after a complaint and police FIR linked to an alleged violation of the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025.

Why it matters: No action will be taken until the next hearing.

💰 Indian state lenders delay plans to sell dollar debt

Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank and Bank of India had planned to raise up to $2.5 billion through dollar debt under the RBI’s subsidised hedging facility, but they paused the sales. Higher US yields, a weaker rupee and more expensive hedging have made dollar fundraising costlier than rupee borrowing.

Why it matters: Higher funding costs are pushing some state lenders to wait before tapping the overseas market.
