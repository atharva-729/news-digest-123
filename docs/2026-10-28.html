🤖 Simplismart raises $14 million for AI inference infrastructure

The startup raised $14 million in a Series B led by Dallas Venture Capital, with NVIDIA, Accel, Shastra VC and Micromax participating. Simplismart builds software that helps enterprises deploy and run AI models efficiently, and also helps cloud providers turn GPU capacity into AI services. It says its annualized revenue run rate has grown 14x in six months.

Why it matters: More capital should help it expand across North America and other markets as demand grows for cheaper AI model execution.

💹 Jio Platforms lists 21% above issue price in stock market debut

📣 Live2.ai raises Rs 130 crore to expand its AI marketing platform

Live2.ai raised Rs 130 crore in a pre-Series B round led by IvyCap Ventures, with some secondary liquidity for early shareholders. The company says it is an AI-based social intelligence and commerce activation platform that helps brands track online conversations, manage campaigns, handle compliance and turn content into shoppable experiences. It works with brands including Schwarzkopf, Salonory, Titan Brands and Shiseido.

Why it matters: The funding should help it scale with enterprise and global consumer brands outside India.

⚡ Tech

🏥 Cent raises $15 million and buys AI radiology platform Medsee

Cent, the preventive healthcare startup founded by Practo co-founder Shashank ND, raised $15 million in a Series A led by Blume Ventures and South Park Commons. It also acquired Medsee, an AI radiology platform. Cent uses imaging, biomarkers and AI for early disease detection, and says about 10,000 people have used its screening protocol so far.

Why it matters: The cash and acquisition should help Cent push its screening model and clinical research across its own centers.

🐟 Scientists count 2.8 million eels around a Tasmania seamount

Researchers led by CSIRO marine scientist Ben Scoulding estimated that up to 2.8 million basketwork eels gather around Patience Seamount off southeast Australia. The eels appear to assemble there for reproduction, and the mountain is south of Tasmania in the Huon Marine Park.

Why it matters: The study puts a hard number on one of the deep sea's biggest fish gatherings.

🛰️ Mystery metal object in Minnesota may be a rocket part

A farmer found an 11-foot-wide bowl-shaped metal object in a Minnesota cornfield, and federal officials took it to a secure location. Experts suspect it may be an upper stage from a LandSpace Zhuque-2E rocket, which launched satellites for a Chinese broadband constellation.

Why it matters: Investigators are trying to confirm whether the debris came from a rocket launch.
