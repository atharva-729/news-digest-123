📣 Live2.ai raises Rs 130 crore to expand its AI marketing tools

🩺 Cent raises $15 million and buys AI radiology startup Medsee

Cent, founded by Practo co-founder Shashank ND, raised $15 million in a Series A co-led by Blume Ventures and South Park Commons. Cent runs preventive health screenings using imaging, biomarkers and AI, and says about 10,000 people have gone through its protocol in the past year. It also acquired Medsee, an AI radiology platform, to strengthen its AI work.

Why it matters: The company plans to use the money for hardware, AI and clinical research to scale early disease detection.

🎈 Fun

🐟 Scientists count 2.8 million eels around an Australian seamount

Researchers studying Patience Seamount off southeast Australia say up to 2.8 million basketwork eels gather there each year. The seamount is a small undersea mountain south of Tasmania, and the scientists think the gathering is linked to reproduction. They used sonar and camera footage to estimate the scale.

Why it matters: The estimate makes this one of the largest deep-ocean fish gatherings recorded.

No pattern yet Original: ScienceAlert ↗
🎈 Fun

🛰️ Mysterious 11-foot object in Minnesota may be rocket debris

Authorities found a shiny, bowl-shaped object in a cornfield in Scott Township, Minnesota, after a farmer spotted it with a combine. Experts think it may be part of a Chinese LandSpace Zhuque-2E rocket, whose upper stage is 11 feet wide. Officials say it has been secured and poses no danger.

Why it matters: Investigators are trying to confirm the debris’s origin and source.
