💊 India cuts prices on 543 anti-cancer medicine brands

The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority has revised the maximum retail prices of 543 anti-cancer brands, with new rates taking effect on November 9. Some cuts are steep: Dr Reddy’s Azacytin falls from ₹6,337 to ₹1,495.04, and RPG Life Sciences’ Anarzole from ₹695.76 to ₹73 for 10 tablets. The list covers different strengths and pack sizes, and will be updated as more company data comes in.

Why it matters: Cancer patients could see lower out-of-pocket costs as trade margins on selected anti-cancer medicines are capped.
