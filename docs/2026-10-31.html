🔐 MeitY proposes consent-based data sharing for AI training

India’s IT ministry has proposed DEPA for AI training, a framework for sharing datasets with consent while keeping raw data private. It is aimed at startups, businesses and academic institutions that want large India-specific datasets to train AI models. The plan uses secure data environments, electronic contracts and privacy tools to keep access traceable and compliant with the DPDP Act.

Why it matters: Gives AI builders a legal path to use sensitive Indian data without handing over the underlying datasets.
