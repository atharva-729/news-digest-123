📄 MeitY releases DEPA handbook for trusted data sharing in AI training

India’s Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology released a handbook on Data Empowerment and Protection Architecture, or DEPA, for AI training. The framework is meant to let institutions share data while keeping control through contracts, secure computing environments and privacy safeguards. IT Secretary S Krishnan unveiled it in Bengaluru with support from the iSPIRT Foundation.

Why it matters: Gives India a formal framework for sharing data used to train AI systems.

🧠 Therapy that reframes breakup pain shows early promise for depression

Researchers in Romania tested a two-day group therapy called cognitive evolutionary therapy, which tries to reduce self-blame by linking breakup pain and other distress to human needs for closeness and safety. In a trial of 61 adults with depressive symptoms, people in therapy improved more than a waiting list group after one week. The benefit was smaller and no longer statistically significant at three months.

Why it matters: A brief group treatment may help depressive symptoms quickly, but the effect still needs larger confirmation.

No pattern yet Original: ScienceAlert ↗
🎈 Fun

🧊 Study says human warming may have interrupted a new ice age

A study in Science suggests the next ice age may have started about 2,000 years ago, then been cut short by human-driven climate change. The work focuses on the Laurentide Ice Sheet and the Barnes Ice Cap in Canada, using beryllium-10 in rocks and carbon-14 in dead vegetation to reconstruct ice history. The researchers say recent summer warming reversed the cap’s growth.

Why it matters: Human warming may have altered the long natural cycle that would have pushed Earth toward another glaciation.
