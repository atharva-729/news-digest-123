🎈 Fun

🔭 Study says early Solar System favored rocky chondrules over icy dust

A Nature Astronomy study used iron meteorites to reconstruct what the earliest solid bodies in the Solar System were made of. The researchers say the first million years were dominated by chondrules, tiny heat-formed rock fragments, not icy matrix material rich in water and organics. They estimate early outer Solar System bodies were 83 percent to 92 percent chondrules.

Why it matters: The findings change how scientists think planets and moons assembled from the start.

🎈 Fun

🧠 Study finds some people with aphantasia dream in images anyway

A Scientific Reports study examined how 205 people’s waking imagination matched their dreams, including 84 with visual aphantasia, the inability to picture things on demand. Nearly half of the aphantasia group said they always had visual experiences in dreams, while others said they never did. The links between waking and dreaming were stronger for sounds and inner speech than for images.

Why it matters: Some people can lose visual imagery while awake yet still have it during sleep.
